Note 2

Operation Hope

I started Operation Hope in March 2024 as a student-led club in Coimbatore. We make cards by hand, write messages of hope inside them, and take them with fruit to patients in government and private hospitals and at a cancer care centre.

I run card-making sessions in schools so that younger students take part, and I work with each hospital to get permission for our visits and the consent of the patients we meet.
